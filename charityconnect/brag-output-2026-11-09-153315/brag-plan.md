# Brag Plan: CharityConnect

## What is this app?
CharityConnect brings donors, charities, and administrators together to discover campaigns, track contributions, run fundraisers, and review activity.

## The angle
A polished, cursor-led tour from the public front door through the three role-based workspaces: find a cause, follow a clearly labelled demo donation, then see the tools behind it. The video treats the real product UI and its small-act tagline as the story, not as a generic SaaS pitch.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on CharityConnect's mint-and-teal home page. Pair the source headline “Support a cause. Make a difference.” with the actual “Small acts. Lasting change.” tagline, then guide a cursor to “Explore campaigns.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- Search and filter campaign cards, with category labels, verified-charity marks, funding progress, and a quick glimpse of charity details and trust information.
- Open a campaign, choose a contribution, and follow the explicitly simulated payment through to the donor's donation history.
- Move from donor tools to charity tools: account/profile and notifications, then campaign creation, campaign updates, and shareable fundraiser links.
- Finish in the admin workspace: review queues, user/charity/campaign management, donations, and reports.

## Outro / punchline
Resolve the admin approval into the CharityConnect mark and the site's own line: “Small acts. Lasting change.” Leave the brand and CTA still long enough to read.

## User flow worth showing
Public home → discover and filter a verified campaign → inspect its details → choose an amount and simulate a donation → see the donation record; then tour charity campaign creation/sharing and admin review/reporting. Interactions in the video are timeline-simulated; the exported video itself is not clickable.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: professional guided tour with real UI language and a visible cursor
- Interpretation: clean feature chapters, measured movement, short purposeful holds, and understated transitions; let the source interface carry the personality.

## Audio direction
- Role: optimistic, polished bed with restrained interaction accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`; bright, warm, and clean
- Music treatment: start under the first frame, moderate-low level, gentle fade in and out; use chapter changes as the main rhythm rather than cutting on every beat.
- Music cue guidance: preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`, approximately 109.96 BPM. Use 20.19s for the admin approval action and 22.37s for the final logo reveal; treat cues as optional. Beat-grid windows around 3.01, 7.35, 11.47, 15.28, and 19.64s suit chapter changes. Keep readable text holds independent of the beat.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; keep the interface steady and let the cursor/clicks communicate action.
- SFX posture: sparse, soft UI clicks on the few clear cursor actions and one warm confirmation accent; no click-track.
- Audio-coupled moments: campaign CTA, amount selection, simulated payment confirmation, charity campaign action, admin approval, logo reveal.
- Restraint rule: no real-payment implication, no rapid-fire ticks, and no sound over the final brand hold.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The public front door — 3.01s
Show the source-faithful CharityConnect home page in a clean browser frame. Keep both lines verbatim: “Small acts. Lasting change.” and “Support a cause. Make a difference.” Pair them with the campaign image and impact card; keep the hero concise so the CTA is easy to follow.
Sequential/interaction: yes — hero settles first, then the cursor clicks the real CTA label; the next scene continues the journey.
Audio intent: warm opening, one soft click on the CTA.
Audio-coupled idea: cursor click and clean transition, not a text typing effect.
Music: upbeat business-pop bed from frame 0.
Transition mood: smooth slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Discover and compare — 4.34s
Show campaign and charity discovery together: search field, cause filters, verified-charity mark, progress bars, and three image-led campaign cards. Briefly reveal the campaign detail/about-trust navigation so the tour communicates where to learn more.
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor selects a cause filter, then one campaign card lifts into focus; keep labels readable as the result set settles.
Audio intent: bright, confident forward motion.
Audio-coupled idea: soft click on the filter, quiet card reveal on the selected campaign.
Music: continue the same bed.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Give with clarity — 4.12s
Open the selected campaign detail: charity verification, campaign progress, and a short description. Move into the amount selector and payment confirmation. Keep “SIMULATED” and “No real money moves” visibly attached to the demo payment; show a successful visual confirmation, not a real transaction claim.
Sequential/interaction: yes — select an amount, click “Continue,” then confirm the explicitly simulated outcome.
Audio intent: calm, reassuring.
Audio-coupled idea: two restrained clicks and one warm confirmation accent; leave the demo label on screen.
Music: continue, slightly under the confirmation cue.
Transition mood: soft wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Your giving, organized — 3.81s
Show the donor workspace: dashboard summary, “My donations” with status/receipt treatment, profile completion, and notifications. Use fictional generic account labels only; do not show email addresses or personal data.
Sequential/interaction: yes — sidebar selection moves from dashboard to donation history, then briefly highlights profile/notifications.
Audio intent: settled and useful.
Audio-coupled idea: one menu click and a status badge reveal.
Music: continue at a steady level.
Transition mood: smooth slide → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — From idea to fundraiser — 4.36s
Show the charity workspace: campaign overview, “Create campaign” form, a managed campaign/update row, and the share-link panel. Use a single charity dashboard frame with quick in-place page swaps so all four tasks are legible.
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor opens the campaign form, submits the visual step, then reveals the share-link action; no claim that a real campaign is published.
Audio intent: capable and constructive.
Audio-coupled idea: click on “Create campaign,” then a light confirmation when the share panel appears.
Music: continue with a subtle lift into the last chapter.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Review, report, repeat — 4.92s
Show the admin dashboard and its charity/campaign approval queues, user and donation navigation, and a compact report view. The cursor approves a pending demo item; replace its pending badge with “Approved.” At 22.37s, transition to the CharityConnect brand and hold “Small acts. Lasting change.” with a clear final CTA through the end.
Sequential/interaction: yes — approval and report view, then final logo/tagline reveal; the final lockup holds for about 2.2s.
Audio intent: reassuring resolution, then a soft brand landing.
Audio-coupled idea: approval on the 20.19s cue; logo entrance near 22.37s; no sound after the final accent.
Music: resolve the existing bed with a short fade.
Transition mood: soft brand dissolve → end

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, polished, human
**Audio summary:** A light, warm bed carries the tour while a few real-feeling UI clicks punctuate the route and the final lockup lands quietly.
